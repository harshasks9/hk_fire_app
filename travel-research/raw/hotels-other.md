# Luxury romantic-workation hotels: Langkawi, Phu Quoc, Bali, Cambodian coast

**Trip:** 2 adults (a couple), Fri 18 Dec to Thu 24 Dec 2026, 6 nights. Vegetarian, eggs OK.
**Schedule:** Sat 19 and Sun 20 Dec are free. Mon 21 to Wed 23 Dec are full remote workdays, and both people may be on video calls at the same time. On Thu 24 Dec they fly on to Siem Reap (SAI).
**Retrieval date for every fact below: 2026-10-21** (unless a different date is noted).

**Confidence key**
- **V1** = official source or live data (the hotel's own site, or a live Expedia tool result).
- **V2** = credible secondary source from the last 12 months, or an undated listing or review that looks current.
- **E** = estimate or inference, labelled as such.
- **U** = unknown or insufficient evidence.

**Method notes and caveats**
- **Expedia prices** come from `mcp__Expedia__search_hotels` (2 adults, 18–24 Dec 2026, retrieved 2026-10-21).
  - `total_price` is the 6-night total in USD, and Expedia states it includes taxes and fees.
  - Expedia's `avg_nightly_price` is *inconsistent*: for Bali and Vietnam it is clearly pre-tax (avg × 6 is below the total), while for Malaysia it is close to the total. For that reason the tables use **total ÷ 6** as the tax-inclusive nightly figure.
  - **Expedia does not show which room category is priced.** The price is almost certainly the cheapest available unit, *not* the recommended work-friendly category, so treat every Expedia total as a **floor**.
  - "Not returned" means the tool did not return the hotel. That is not proof the hotel has no availability.
- **WebFetch failed with DNS errors** on fourseasons.com and media.sixsenses.com, so each was tried only once. Every other fact comes from WebSearch snippets.
- **Internet evidence is very thin for every property.** No 2025–26 review with Mbps figures or Zoom/Teams experience was found for any shortlisted hotel. "Free Wi-Fi" listings are *not* counted as evidence. Where nothing credible exists, the entry says **"insufficient evidence"**.
- **Mobile signal:** no property-specific evidence was found for any hotel (U throughout).

## 2. Destination-level context

### Langkawi (Malaysia)
- **December weather.** Sources conflict.
  - Upbeat view: Rough Guides calls December one of Langkawi's best beach months, and Selective Asia describes "plenty of sunshine" on the west coast (V2). [roughguides.com/malaysia/when-to-go/december](https://www.roughguides.com/malaysia/when-to-go/december/), [selectiveasia.com](https://www.selectiveasia.com/malaysia-holidays/weather/december)
  - Cautious view: bestdateweather.com still counts December as "wet season", with about 55% rainy days and brief, intense showers (V2). [bestdateweather.com](https://bestdateweather.com/us/langkawi-weather-december)
  - Sea temperature is 25–29 °C (V2). Datai Bay and Tanjung Rhu are on the north and north-west coasts, sheltered from the north-east monsoon that hits Peninsular Malaysia's east coast. **Calm swimming is likely but not verified per bay (E).**
- **Internet context.**
  - A digital-nomad guide says "100mbps+" is not unusual in coworking spaces or high-end hotels (V2, undated). [danroundtheworld.com](https://danroundtheworld.com/langkawi-digital-nomad-guide/)
  - A TripAdvisor forum warns Wi-Fi "can be very patchy" depending on weather (V2, undated). [tripadvisor forum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g298283-i8829-k9783890-What_s_the_wifi_like_in_Langkawi-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html)
  - For comparison only, a review of the *Westin* Langkawi measured 9.12 Mbps down and 8.46 Mbps up in the room, about 4 years ago. That is a different hotel and old data, so it counts as context only (E). [thepointsguy.com](https://thepointsguy.com/hotel/reviews/westin-langkawi-resort-spa/)
- **Weekend experiences** (E, general knowledge, no source this session): Kilim Geoforest mangrove boat tour (near Tanjung Rhu), SkyCab cable car and Sky Bridge at Machinchang (closer to Datai and Pantai Kok), island-hopping, sunset cruises.

### Phu Quoc (Vietnam)
- **December sea conditions.** December is in the dry, north-east-monsoon season.
  - **West coast** (Long Beach / Bai Truong, where Regent and InterContinental sit): the sea is "so calm it looks more like a lake", with good to excellent swimming from Nov to Mar (V2). [vietnamtourism.com](https://www.vietnamtourism.com/en/phu-quoc-when-to-visit-for-the-calmest-seas)
  - **East coast:** "takes the full force of wind and swell", with swimming rated unsuitable from Nov to Mar (same source, V2). Bai Khem (JW Marriott, New World) is on the **south-east** side (Expedia coordinates 10.034N, 104.028E). Other sources describe Khem as calm and gentle, but without dates (V2). This is **conflicting evidence**: treat it as a risk (E).
- **Construction is heavy and peaking.**
  - Phu Quoc hosts **APEC 2027**. An Giang province is pushing **21 major projects** with a **31 May 2027 deadline** (V2, Sept 2026). [vietnamnews.vn 1800651](https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1800651/phu-quoc-races-to-complete-apec-2027-infrastructure.html), [news.tuoitre.vn](https://news.tuoitre.vn/saigontimes/may-2027-deadline-set-for-apec-infrastructure-on-phu-quoc-island-105554110.htm)
  - Projects include: the airport's second runway, Terminal 2 and VIP terminal ("some facilities expected to open... before December 30, 2026"); APEC Boulevard and Provincial Road 975 (due end-2026); light rail; and the **APEC Convention Centre at Bai Dat Do** (Sun Group, about 16 ha) (V2). [vietnamplus](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/apec-2027-convention-centre-takes-shape-in-phu-quoc-post349797.vnp)
  - Workers skipped the 2026 New Year break to speed things up (V2). [news.tuoitre.vn](https://news.tuoitre.vn/workers-skip-new-year-break-to-speed-up-apec-2027-projects-in-vietnam-103260103172859036.htm)
  - **Expect construction traffic, dust and possible noise in Dec 2026**, especially near the airport (Bai Truong) and the south (An Thoi, Bai Khem) (E).
  - An Oct 2025 review of another resort (S-Solution) reported pile-driving from 7 am that woke guests through closed windows (V2, different property). A 2026 overview flags construction and rubbish outside resort zones (V2). [vietnamspot.ru](https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/phu-quoc-reviews)
- **Internet context.**
  - The island has no coworking spaces, and internet speeds are "inconsistent" (V2). [thebrokebackpacker.com](https://www.thebrokebackpacker.com/where-to-stay-in-phu-quoc-vietnam/)

### Bali (Indonesia)
- **December is wet season. Confirmed.**
  - Bali.com gives about **22 rain days and 32 cm of rain** island-wide, with Ubud and the highlands getting "frequent, prolonged" showers. Uluwatu gets less rain, and its showers dry quickly (V2). [bali.com](https://bali.com/travel-guide/weather-by-month/weather-december/)
  - Pick Your Trail gives Ubud 18–22 rainy days and Uluwatu 12–16 (V2). Weatherapi's Ubud history averages about 23 rain days and roughly 324 mm (V2). **Sources conflict on magnitude, but all agree Ubud is wetter.**
  - Rain tends to fall in afternoons and evenings (V2). Peak holiday demand runs from about **23 Dec to 6 Jan** (V2). [baliuntold.com](https://baliuntold.com/visit/best-time-to-visit/bali-in-december/)
- **Beaches in December:** this is the west and south-west coast **"trash season" (Dec–Mar)**, with plastic washing up on Kuta, Legian, Seminyak, **Jimbaran** and Canggu, and the stretch running from Uluwatu to Canggu (V2, older reporting from 2018–2021). [canberratimes.com.au](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/5144296/bali-beaches-buried-in-rubbish-as-indonesia-battles-oceans-of-plastic/) Uluwatu cliff resorts have little or no swimmable beach anyway (E). **For Bali in December, plan around the private pool, not the sea.**
- **Festive rules.**
  - A guide says Christmas week often carries a **5–7-night minimum** at luxury properties (V2, generic). [hotelierschoice.com](https://hotelierschoice.com/fr/guide/bali/christmas/)
  - Four Seasons Jimbaran lists **group** blackout dates from 20 Dec 2026 to 7 Jan 2027 (V1, groups only).
  - No compulsory gala dinner was found. Four Seasons Sayan's Christmas Eve gala is described as optional and "coveted", and is moot because the couple departs on 24 Dec.
- **Transfers:** Denpasar airport to Ubud/Sayan is about 75–90 min, and to Uluwatu or Jimbaran about 20–60 min. Both are **E, not verified** (search returned nothing on traffic).
- **Onward on 24 Dec:** DPS to SAI has no direct flight found. A connection via KUL, SIN or BKK is likely and is the longest of the four destinations (E).

### Cambodian coast
- **December sea:** Koh Rong is best from mid-Dec to early Mar, with "calm seas". Sihanoukville has "little to no rain" and the sea is "usually calm and clear" (V2). The same guide warns that rough seas "regularly disrupt" the longer **ferries** to Koh Rong and Koh Rong Samloem (V2). [championtraveler.com](https://championtraveler.com/dates/best-time-to-visit-koh-rong-kh/) Krabey's 15-minute private crossing carries lower risk (E).
- **Onward on 24 Dec:** **Cambodia Airways reportedly runs a daily Sihanoukville (KOS) to Siem Reap (SAI) rotation from 26 Jun 2026** (V2). [goasia.cc](https://goasia.cc/en/news/cambodia-domestic-flights-phnom-penh-siem-reap-sihanoukville) It is also about 3 h by road to Phnom Penh (V2), then a roughly 51-minute KTI to SAI flight (V2, Skyscanner). This is **the simplest 24 Dec connection of the four destinations, if the December schedule holds (verify).**

## 6. Pre-booking checklist (ask each shortlisted hotel in writing)
1. **Measured Wi-Fi in the specific villa or suite:** download and upload Mbps, whether it is wired or mesh, and whether the backhaul is fibre. Can they guarantee two simultaneous HD video calls? Is an Ethernet port or a portable router available? Is there a backup 4G/5G router?
2. Is the **living room enclosed and air-conditioned**, with a proper desk and ergonomic chair? Can they set up a second desk in the bedroom?
3. **Construction or noise** within 1 km in Dec 2026, especially at Phu Quoc properties.
4. Festive **minimum stay** or compulsory supplements for 18–24 Dec. None was found for any shortlisted hotel (U).
5. **Vegetarian menus** in writing for Datai, Four Seasons Langkawi, St. Regis, Regent, JW, Four Seasons Sayan and Mandapa (no evidence found).
6. Cancellation and breakfast terms. Expedia does not show these through this tool, so get direct quotes.
7. Bring a local eSIM or data plan as a backup connection (mobile signal at every property is U).

# Destination Screen: Luxury Romantic Workation in SE Asia (Phase 1: Dec 18-24, 2026)

Prepared 2026-10-21 by the destination research analyst. This is a screening document. Flight schedules and hotel rates go to separate analysts.

## 0. Method, assumptions and confidence legend

**Trip constraints used for screening**
- Couple, 2 adults. **Assumption:** both hold ordinary **Indian passports**. Where it differs, Singapore residence or PR is noted in section 2.3.
- Departure is from Singapore (SIN) or Hyderabad (HYD) on Fri Dec 18, 2026. Sat-Sun Dec 19-20 are free days. Mon-Wed Dec 21-23 are full remote workdays with video calls.
- On **Thu Dec 24, 2026** (weekday checked) they fly to Siem Reap Angkor International Airport (**SAI**) to meet family, who come from HYD.
- Food is vegetarian, eggs OK.

**Sourcing**
- Every source below was retrieved on **2026-10-21**. Almost everything comes from WebSearch result snippets.
- WebFetch failed with a DNS error on newdelhi.thaiembassy.org and en.wikipedia.org, so no primary page was opened in full.
- An unanswered search is recorded as **U**. It is not evidence that something is absent.

**Confidence legend**
- **V1:** official or primary source (government, met service, airline or hotel's own page). Seen via a search snippet here.
- **V2:** credible secondary source dated within about 12 months.
- **E:** analyst estimate or general knowledge. Not sourced in this session; verify it.
- **U:** unknown or unconfirmed.

**Elimination criteria** (applied in section 3)
- **C1 Weather (hard fail):** December falls in the active wet or monsoon season on that coast. That means an official or consensus wet-season classification, mean December rain above about 200 mm, or recent December flooding.
- **C2 Connectivity (hard fail):** more than one connection to reach SAI on Thu Dec 24, **and** long or awkward access from SIN/HYD.
- **C3 Visa friction (flag):** a pre-arranged visa or embassy visa is needed, there are multiple forms or levies, or the policy is in flux.
- **C4 Workation infrastructure (flag; fail if severe):** no evidence of reliable broadband or 5G, a known power or cable fragility, or remote resorts.
- **C5 Luxury inventory (hard fail if weak):** few or no internationally benchmarked 5-star or ultra-luxury resorts.
- **C6 Romance fit (flag):** a city with no beach or nature-retreat element.
- **C7 Peak-season hazards (flag):** floods, jellyfish, rip currents, haze, compulsory gala dinners.

**Monsoon logic (E, consistent with the sources above):**
- In December the **Andaman coast** (Phuket, Krabi, Phang Nga, Khao Lak, and Langkawi to a lesser degree) is in its NE-monsoon dry season.
- The **Gulf of Thailand** islands (Samui) are at the tail of their Oct-Dec rains.
- **Central Vietnam** (Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang) is at the end of its Sep-Dec flood season.
- **Bali** and **Singapore** are in their wet seasons.
- **Mainland Indochina** (Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Luang Prabang, Siem Reap) is at its driest.

### 2.2 Totals of entry friction for Phase 1, before Cambodia (E, derived from the table)
- **Thailand: zero fee.** Just the TDAC.
- **Malaysia: zero fee.** Just the MDAC.
- **Vietnam:** US$25 e-visa (recommended even for Phu Quoc).
- **Bali:** IDR 500k e-VOA (possibly 750k), plus IDR 150k levy, plus the All Indonesia form.
- **Singapore:** visa needed if coming from HYD.
- **Cambodia:** everyone needs the US$30 Cambodia visa and e-Arrival anyway for Phase 2. Doing Phase 1 in Cambodia (Siem Reap or the coast) avoids a second country's formalities.

### 2.3 If the couple are Singapore residents or PR
- **Singapore itself:** no visa needed.
- **Elsewhere:** no residence-based waivers were found for Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Cambodia or Laos. An unanswered search is not proof that none exist.
- **Malaysia MDAC:** Singapore PR status reportedly does **not** exempt you from the MDAC. Only Singapore citizens are exempt. Source: [Wise](https://wise.com/sg/blog/malaysia-visa) (V2). Older reporting ([NST](https://www.nst.com.my/node/986785/amp)) suggested a PR exemption under an earlier rule, which conflicts; Wise is more recent.
- **For the flight analyst:** from Hyderabad, a SIN routing is only airside transit and needs no Singapore visa if they stay airside (E).

**Trip:** Fri 18 Dec to Thu 24 Dec 2026 (6 nights), 2 adults, vegetarian (eggs OK). Sat 19 and Sun 20 Dec are free days. Mon 21 to Wed 23 Dec are full remote workdays, and both people may be on video calls at the same time. On Thu 24 Dec they fly on to Siem Reap (SAI, Siem Reap Angkor International).
**Researched:** 2026-10-21. All retrieval dates are 2026-10-21 unless stated otherwise.

## Confidence key and method

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **V1** | Official or live source: the hotel or brand site, or a live Expedia API result on 2026-10-21 |
| **V2** | Credible secondary source (OTA listing, press, review site, guide). Some are older than 12 months; where that matters it is flagged |
| **E** | Analyst estimate or general destination knowledge, not verified by a source in this session |
| **U** | Unknown, or insufficient evidence |

- **Expedia pricing.** Every search used `mcp__Expedia__search_hotels` with 2026-12-18 to 2026-12-24 and 2 adults. `total_price` is the 6-night total in USD, which Expedia says includes taxes and fees. Expedia's displayed "avg nightly" is lower than total ÷ 6. The gap is about +17.7% in Thailand, which matches 10% service charge plus 7% VAT, and about +20% in Laos (E, inferred). Both figures are therefore shown.
- **Expedia does not show the room category.** Each total is the lowest rate Expedia offered, so it is almost certainly the entry category. **The suites recommended below will cost more.** Confirm suite rates directly with each hotel.
- **"Not returned"** means Expedia did not return the hotel for these dates. That does not prove the hotel has no availability.
- **WebFetch** was tried once (easia-travel.com) and failed with a DNS error, so everything else relies on WebSearch snippets.
- **Wi-Fi.** "Free Wi-Fi" in a listing is not treated as evidence that the connection is reliable. **No source found in this session gives an independent measured speed or a guest report about video calls for any shortlisted hotel.** The best evidence available is one speed figure reported by the property itself (Four Seasons Bangkok).

### 0.2 Koh Samui December weather (requested check)

Sources conflict. December is wetter than the dry months and drier than November. Rain tapers off through the month.

- One source gives **210.3 mm and 15 rain days** ([Amazing Lanta](https://amazinglanta.com/koh-samui-weather/)).
- Others give about 116–153 mm and 9–16 rain days ([Avionero](https://avionero.com/New-York-Ko-Samui.NYC-USM/weather/december), [TUI](https://www.tui.co.uk/holidays/weather/asia/thailand/thailand/koh-samui/december.html)).
- WeatherSpark puts the chance of a wet day at **49% on 1 Dec, falling to 27% by 31 Dec** ([WeatherSpark](https://weatherspark.com/m/112991/12/Average-Weather-in-December-in-Ko-Samui-Thailand)).
- The rainy season is described as roughly Oct–Dec ([Koh Samui Guide](https://www.thekohsamuiguide.com/koh-samui-best-time/)).
- Confidence: V2. A realistic expectation is about 100–210 mm and about 15 wet days, mostly short downpours. For two free beach days this is a real risk, and it is one reason Samui is screened out.

V2; the press release is promotional.

**Chiang Mai**
- **Sukjai by Pata Obasan** is a vegetarian restaurant listed by Michelin, in a restored Lanna house on Charoen Muang Rd ([Chiang Mai CityLife, 2025](https://www.chiangmaicitylife.com/clg/living/from-street-eats-to-fine-dining-who-made-the-chiang-mai-michelin-cut-in-2025/), V2).

- I could not verify a claim that a restaurant called "Mina" is a Michelin-starred vegetarian restaurant; the only source was a tour operator (U).

**Luang Prabang**
- **Reunion Café** (fully vegan), **Chepa Phon Phan** (vegan Lao food), **The Happy Cat** and **Two Little Birds** (vegetarian cafés), from [HappyCow](https://www.happycow.net/reviews/reunion-cafe-luang-prabang-503378).
- **L'Éléphant Vert** serves vegetarian and vegan tasting menus, with lunch from about 120,000 kip ([CityNomads](https://citynomads.com/restaurant-review-lelephant-vert-in-luang-prabang-laos/); the review is undated).
- V2, with mixed dates. Check that each place is still open.

### 0.5 Luang Prabang internet (destination level)

- Reports are mixed. One guide says the connection is "slower and less reliable than Vientiane; video calls can be challenging". Others say cafés reach about 65 Mbps, which is "plenty for video calls".
- Speeds reported on Nomads.com (about 4–5 Mbps) conflict with a fixed-broadband figure of about 34–35 Mbps.
- A **Unitel SIM** is the usual mobile-data backup recommendation. Coverage is thinner outside town. Power cuts are mentioned.
- Sources: [internetlaos](https://shop.internetlaos.com/blog/is-there-internet-in-luang-prabang-wifi-data-guide) (an eSIM vendor, so treat as marketing), [Nomads](https://nomads.com/digital-nomad-guide/luang-prabang), [4stogo](https://4stogo.com/blog/posts/remote-work-and-wi-fi-cafes), [Stamped Nomad](https://www.stampednomad.com/cities/luang-prabang).
- Overall confidence: V2/U. **Treat this as a structural risk for two people on simultaneous calls.**

## 2. Deep dives: the best 6

### 2.1 Four Seasons Hotel Bangkok at Chao Phraya River (ranked #1)

**Basics**
- **Brand:** Four Seasons; no points program. Opened 2020 (E).
- **Area:** Charoenkrung riverside, next to Capella.
- **Transfer from BKK:** about 45–75 min by car, depending on traffic (E).

**Recommended room: Executive Suite, about 110 m² (1,184 sq ft)**
- River view, a separate bedroom and a dining table ([Expedia/Hotels.com listing](https://widgets.hotels.com/ho1168242624/four-seasons-hotel-bangkok-at-chao-phraya-river-bangkok-thailand), V2). One person can take calls in the living room while the other uses the bedroom.
- Fallback: the Studio Riverview Suite (about 77 m², day bed) is open-plan and **not** suitable for two simultaneous callers.

**Desk:** room listings include a desk, desk chair, adapters and chargers (V2). Ergonomic chair U.

**Private pool:** no (shared resort-style pools; E).

**Internet**
- Expedia/Hotels.com lists in-room Wi-Fi at **100+ Mbps, "suits 1–2 people or up to 6 devices"** (V2, **reported by the property, not measured**).
- No guest review mentioning Zoom or Teams was found. **Independent evidence is insufficient.**
- Mobile signal: Bangkok 4G/5G is generally strong (E).
- Ask the hotel whether a wired Ethernet port is available in the suite.

**Noise:** set back from Charoenkrung Rd with a garden and river frontage. River boat horns are possible (E).

**Vegetarian dining**
- On property: no published vegetarian menu was found (U). Four Seasons kitchens generally accommodate vegetarians (E).
- Nearby: Bangkok's deep vegetarian scene (§0.4). Côte at Capella next door reportedly welcomes vegetarians (V2).

**Romance:** riverfront setting, spa (E), a 2025 holiday programme of afternoon tea and New Year events ([FS press](https://press.fourseasons.com/bangkok/hotel-news/2025/holiday-offerings), V2).

**Weekend ideas (E):** khlong (canal) longtail tour, Wat Arun and Wat Pho by river boat, Chatuchak weekend market, sunset rooftop bar.

**Reviews:** Expedia 9.8/10 from 389 reviews (V1). No negative 2025–26 pattern was found, but no detailed recent reviews were retrieved either (U).

**Price**
- Expedia: **$5,953 total** ($992/night including taxes; displayed $843), entry category, V1, 2026-10-21. The Executive Suite will be higher (U).
- Festive minimum stay, breakfast and cancellation terms for 2026: U. Ask the hotel.

**Deal-breakers:** only if the Executive Suite rate is out of budget. A single-room category would fail the simultaneous-calls requirement.

### Score justifications

**1. Four Seasons Bangkok**
- **Romance 8:** riverfront with gardens and spa. Urban, with no private pool.
- **Work 8:** the only shortlisted hotel with any speed figure (100+ Mbps). It is reported by the property, not measured, so this score is only medium confidence. Desk and chair are listed.
- **Room 9:** the 110 m² suite has a separate bedroom and living room, so two people can be on calls at once.
- **Logistics 9:** BKK has several non-stop SAI flights a day, so a morning or afternoon departure on Thursday is easy (V2).
- **Value 6:** the $5,953 entry rate is mid-range for a Bangkok riverfront hotel, but the suite premium is unknown.

**2. Capella Bangkok**
- **Romance 10:** every room faces the river, there are villas with plunge pools, and Côte is on site.
- **Work 7:** large suites with separate living rooms, but no Wi-Fi evidence. The score is lowered for that.
- **Room 9:** suites of 95–209 m² with separate living rooms.
- **Logistics 9:** same as Four Seasons.
- **Value 3:** the highest entry price in Bangkok ($9,813), and the suite needed adds a large premium.

**3. Rosewood Bangkok**
- **Romance 6:** city tower with no river.
- **Work 7:** a 79 m² separate living room. Central CBD with the BTS, and fibre-rich surroundings (E). Wi-Fi itself is U.
- **Room 8:** a confirmed separate living room, but the smallest two-room option of the top three.
- **Logistics 9:** central, and the shortest BKK transfer (E).
- **Value 9:** the $2,865 entry rate (strike-through $4,364) leaves a lot of budget for the suite upgrade.

**4. Four Seasons Resort Chiang Mai**
- **Romance 9:** rice-paddy valley, and the Chiang Mai weekend (Doi Suthep, sanctuary, Sunday Walking Street) is excellent.
- **Work 6:** the 244 m² residence is ideal in space, but there is zero connectivity evidence and the location is rural.
- **Room 9:** but only for the residence category. A pavilion would score 4.
- **Logistics 5:** no non-stop CNX–SAI, so Thursday needs a connection through BKK (V2). The airport is about 30–40 min away (E).
- **Value 5:** no price available. This score is a placeholder with low confidence.

**5. Anantara Chiang Mai**
- **Romance 7:** riverside, colonial-era building, city access.
- **Work 6:** a writing desk is evidenced (V1). Connectivity is U.
- **Room 7:** the serviced One-Bedroom Suite has a confirmed living room, but at 60 m² it is compact. The resort suite's layout is U.
- **Logistics 6:** central and close to CNX, but the SAI route needs a connection.
- **Value 6:** $5,429 for the entry room is high for a city hotel.

**6. Rosewood Luang Prabang**
- **Romance 10:** a remarkable forest and waterfall setting, plus alms-giving and Kuang Si at the weekend.
- **Work 4:** Luang Prabang connectivity is mixed (V2), the hotel has no evidence (U), and the location is rural.
- **Room 4:** single-space tents and villas, with no confirmed second room.
- **Logistics 8:** VN931 LPQ→SAI on Thursday at 14:45, with a 17-min transfer, has to be re-verified.
- **Value 4:** $8,697 for small units.

### Runners-up and notes
- **Aman Nai Lert Bangkok** could rank in the top 3 if two things are confirmed: (a) the Deluxe or Premier Suites (92+ m²) have a door between the living room and bedroom, and (b) Wi-Fi quality. Its rates of $1,200–1,600+/night include breakfast and limousine transfer (V2). It is not on Expedia.
- **Sindhorn Kempinski** ($2,802; 66 m² entry rooms with a sitting area; 9.8 score) is the strongest value fallback in Bangkok.
- **Samui** is excluded because December is the tail of the monsoon (about 15 wet days), Six Senses has partial closures until 30 Nov, there is no USM–SAI non-stop, and Six Senses ($8,629) and Banyan Tree ($7,596) are expensive.
- **Amantaka** has the best town location in Luang Prabang, but its suites are open-plan (V1). That is the weakest layout for two simultaneous callers.

### Questions to send each shortlisted hotel before booking
1. Suite rate for 18–24 Dec, including breakfast, the cancellation deadline and any festive supplement.
2. Wi-Fi bandwidth per room, whether it is a dedicated or shared network, whether there is a wired Ethernet port, and what backup exists in a power cut.
3. Whether an ergonomic chair or second desk can be added in the living room.
4. Late checkout on 24 Dec, and the airport transfer price.
5. Vegetarian (eggs OK) menus at the signature restaurants.

**Recommendation:** Apply for **e-Visas for all 6** (children included, each with their own application) by about **1 Dec 2026**, at least 3 weeks ahead. Complete the **e-Arrival card for each traveller between 17 and 23 Dec**. Print the e-Visas and save the QR codes offline. That avoids a VOA queue at SAI with tired kids after a connection. Fallback: VOA at SAI with USD cash (about 30–35 per person) and photos.

## 9. Day-by-day plan (Thu 24 – Mon 28 Dec 2026)

Basis: Sofitel (or Park Hyatt). Private licensed guide plus A/C van on 25–27 Dec. 3-day Angkor Pass for the 4 adults, kids free with passports. Temple blocks are early morning and late afternoon; pool and rest at midday.

**Thu 24 Dec: Arrival / Christmas Eve**
- Arrive at SAI. Show the e-Visa and e-Arrival QR codes. Van transfer about 1–1h15 (45 km).
- Check in, then pool time.
- If the passes were not bought online, the guide can take the adults to the Road 60 office with each adult's photo (opening hours U).
- 18:30 relaxed dinner: hotel Christmas Eve (check the gala supplement) or **Peace Cafe** (garden play area; cash).
- Lay out temple clothes (shoulders and knees covered) and headlamps. Lights out by 21:00.

**Fri 25 Dec: Angkor Wat sunrise + Ta Prohm; Phare matinee**
- 04:40 depart with hotel breakfast boxes.
- About 05:05 enter Angkor Wat (opens 05:00). North reflection pool for **sunrise about 06:23**.
- 06:45–08:30 explore Angkor Wat while groups leave for breakfast: bas-relief galleries; kids and one adult stay below the Bakan.
- 08:45–09:45 **Ta Prohm** (tree roots, boardwalks).
- About 10:30 back at the hotel: pool, lunch, **nap**.
- 16:15 depart for **Phare Circus 17:00 matinee** (Fri). Section A: adults $38, kids $18.
- 18:30 early dinner nearby (Chusska or Vanakkam India for Indian vegetarian).
- Bed by 20:30.

**Sat 26 Dec: Banteay Srei + Beng Mealea**
- 06:45 depart (sleep-in versus Day 1).
- About 07:45–08:45 **Banteay Srei** (at opening, before the coaches).
- About 1 h drive. 10:00–11:30 **Beng Mealea** jungle ruin (in the Angkor Pass).
- Packed or local vegetarian lunch (guide to brief the kitchen: no fish sauce).
- About 13:30 back. Pool and rest.
- Evening options: **Apsara dinner show** about 18:30–20:30 (about $23–29 per adult; check vegetarian options) or a quiet hotel dinner.
- *Swap option:* **Phnom Kulen** waterfall ($20 per adult, separate ticket) instead of Beng Mealea. Better for a swim, but there are steps for the grandparents.

**Sun 27 Dec: Angkor Thom + Preah Khan; Tonle Sap sunset**
- 07:00 depart. 07:20 **South Gate** causeway.
- 07:30–08:45 **Bayon** faces (early, before mid-morning groups), then Baphuon exterior and the Terrace of the Elephants / Leper King.
- 09:15–10:30 **Preah Khan** (shady labyrinth); optional quick **Neak Pean** walkway.
- Back by 11:00. Lunch at **Banllé** (farm tour for the kids) or the hotel, then a nap.
- 14:30 depart for **Kampong Phluk** (about 1 h): stilt village plus boat or walk depending on water (confirm), and **sunset on Tonle Sap about 17:40**.
- Back about 19:00. Light dinner.
- *Alt:* skip Tonle Sap and do the **Phare 17:00 Sunday matinee** here instead of Friday, or the Angkor Balloon at sunset if it is operating.

**Mon 28 Dec: Departure to Hyderabad**
- Lazy breakfast and a last swim. Optional short visit to the Old Market for souvenirs (E).
- Leave the hotel **at least 4 h before an international departure**: about 1–1h15 transfer plus check-in (E).
- Connect via BKK, SIN or KUL to HYD (routing per the flight research).

**Indicative extras (excluding hotel):** Angkor passes **$248**. Phare Section A **about $188** (4 × $38 + 2 × $18). Guide + van **3 days about $300–540 (E, unsourced; get quotes)**. Airport van × 2 trips U. Apsara dinner about **$92–116 for 4 adults** (kids' rate U). e-Visas **about $216** (6 × $36, V2).

# Andaman Coast luxury hotels: romantic workation shortlist

**Trip:** Fri 18 Dec to Thu 24 Dec 2026 (6 nights), 2 adults, vegetarian (eggs OK).
**Pattern:** Sat 19 and Sun 20 are free days. Mon 21 to Wed 23 are full remote workdays, and both people may be on video calls at the same time. On Thu 24 they fly to Siem Reap, probably via Bangkok.
**Research date:** 2026-10-21. Every source below was retrieved on that date.

**Confidence codes**
- **V1**: official or live data (the hotel's own site, or a live Expedia API result).
- **V2**: a credible secondary source. The code is given only when the source looks current (12 months or less). When a source is older or undated, that is stated.
- **E**: an estimate or inference made by the analyst.
- **U**: unknown, or needs confirmation.

Sources are listed at the end as [S#]. Each fact is tagged `[S#|code]`.

**Method notes**
- **WebFetch failed** with a DNS error (ENOTFOUND) on tripadvisor.com, phuket101.net, dorsiatravel.com and comohotels.com. Each site was tried once, so everything that is not Expedia comes from **WebSearch result snippets**. Wording in those snippets is paraphrased by the search engine, so treat review quotes as V2 at best.
- **Expedia** results come from the `search_hotels` tool: Phuket, Koh Yao, Khao Lak, Krabi or Koh Lanta, 2026-12-18 to 2026-12-24, 2 adults, limit 6, retrieved 2026-10-21. Expedia **does not show the room category**. The price is the cheapest rate on offer for each hotel, which is normally the entry-level room rather than the two-room category recommended below.
- **Expedia price fields:** `total_price` is labelled "Total with taxes and fees". The `avg_nightly_price` field is about 15.7–15.9% lower than total ÷ 6. The likely reason is that it leaves out Thailand's 10% service charge plus 7% VAT (E). **This report uses the total as the authoritative figure** and also shows total ÷ 6.
- **"Free Wi-Fi" in listings is not counted as evidence of reliability.** No source found for any property gives a measured Mbps figure or a test of simultaneous video calls. Internet reliability is therefore **insufficient evidence (U) everywhere**. See the mitigation plan in section 5.
- **Distances** are straight-line km, computed from the Expedia latitude and longitude to HKT (8.1132 N, 98.3169 E) or KBV (8.0992 N, 98.9862 E). They are labelled E. Real road distance is usually 1.3–1.6 times longer.

## 2. Deep dives (best 6)

### 2.1 COMO Point Yamu (Cape Yamu, Phuket)

**Brand and logistics**
- **Brand:** COMO Hotels. Loyalty program: U.
- **Area:** Cape Yamu peninsula on Phuket's east coast, facing Phang Nga Bay.
- **Transfer:** HKT is 18 km straight-line (E), so roughly 30–40 min by road (E). No boat is needed.
- **Location:** remote. Phuket101 says guests need a car or a tour to do much off-site [S20|V2, undated].

**Recommended room: COMO Suite**
- **Size and layout:** 300 m² with **separate living and dining areas** [S19|V1, official FAQ via snippet]. A second listing confirms 300 m² [S24|V2]. One person can take calls in the living room and the other in the bedroom.
- **Smaller option:** Phang Nga Pool Rooms and Suites are 97–180 m² with private pools [S19|V1]. Whether the Pool Suite has a separate living room is **U**.
- **Desk and chair:** no evidence found (U).
- **Private pool:** COMO Suite pool status is U. The Pool Rooms and Suites do have private pools [S19|V1].
- **View:** Phang Nga Bay. One reviewer's suite looked over a "muddy" harbour instead of the bay [S20|V2]. **Request a bay-view suite in writing.**

**Internet**
- **Insufficient evidence (U).** Listings say only that Wi-Fi is available throughout. No speed reports or video-call reports were found.
- **Mobile:** True says it finished modernizing Phuket's network in May 2025 and that 5G signal strength rose 16% [S51|V2, operator claim].

**Beach, noise and surroundings**
- **No beach at the resort.** Phuket101 says the nearest is Bang Tao, about 19 km away [S20|V2]. That source also gives the direction as "east", which is wrong: Bang Tao is on the west coast.
- December sea conditions don't matter here. The main pool is the draw.
- **Noise:** no construction or noise reports were found (U). The cape location suggests it is quiet (E).

**Vegetarian dining**
- **Restaurants:** La Sirena (Italian) and Nahmyaa (Southern Thai), with menus "drawing on the principles of COMO Shambhala Kitchen" [S21|V1].
- **Reviews:** reviews of Nahmyaa focus on meat and seafood. Staff adjust dishes on request [S22|V2].
- **Gap:** no published vegetarian menu was found (U). Italian food plus a wellness kitchen is likely to work for a vegetarian who eats eggs (E).

**Spa, romance and reviews**
- **Spa and experiences:** COMO Shambhala spa (E). Floating breakfast and garden-to-table experiences are listed on the COMO site [S21|V1].
- **Free days:** the bay position suits a Phang Nga Bay boat day on Sat or Sun (E).
- **Expedia rating:** 9.6/10 from 707 reviews [S1|V1].
- **TripAdvisor summary:** recent reviews praise the service, atmosphere and infinity pool. Some guests caution about extra charges [S23|V2].

**Deal-breakers**
- No beach.
- Remote, so you depend on the resort for most meals.
- The cheap Expedia rate is almost certainly a single-room Pool Room, not the COMO Suite.

**Price and terms**
- **Expedia:** **$3,270 total** for 6 nights (÷6 = $545; avg-nightly field $459; strikeout $4,247) [S1|V1, cheapest room, category not shown].
- **COMO Suite:** price U. Expect a multiple of the base rate (E).
- **Breakfast, cancellation and festive rules:** U.

### 2.2 Trisara (Phuket, northwest)

**Brand and logistics**
- **Brand:** independent. Loyalty program: U.
- **Area:** a private cove between Naithon and Layan.
- **Transfer:** HKT is 9.5 km straight-line (E), roughly 15–25 min (E). No boat is needed.
- **Layout:** the resort is built into a steep, jungled hillside. Guests request buggies by WhatsApp chat with the concierge [S9|V2, 2023].

**Recommended room: Signature Ocean View Pool Suite**
- **Size and layout:** about 230 m² (2,476 sq ft), with a **separate living area** that has its own dining area and sofas [S5|V2, OTA listing].
- **Conflicting source:** a 2005 trade report gives a "pool suite" of about 117 m² with a 50 m² bedroom [S6|old, probably a different or earlier category].
- **Desk and work facilities:** no direct photo or description of a desk was found (U). A 2024-era review mentions ample power points (V2, via [S2/S4] snippets). The resort has a business centre and a meeting room [V2, snippet], which could serve as backup workspace.
- **Private pool and view:** private pool, ocean view [S5|V2].

**Internet: mixed evidence, U overall**
- **Positive:** a Luxury Traveller review from 2022 says the Wi-Fi was "stable, fast and free" [S2|V2, more than 12 months old].
- **Negative:** a Dorsia Travel reviewer found the internet "more reliable in the car on the way there" [S3|U, undated]. A TripAdvisor reviewer reported in-room internet not working [S4|U, date unclear].

**Beach, noise and surroundings**
- **Conflicting beach reports:** one guide says the main pool sits "steps from the fine sandy beach" in a secluded cove [S8|V2]. Roomer summarises Trisara as trading "a swimmable beach for absolute privacy" [S7|V2].
- **December seas:** the west coast is generally calm and clear in December [S53|V2]. **Ask the resort about swimming in the cove in late December.**
- **Noise:** no construction reports were found for 2025–26 (U).
- **Isolation:** high. Off-site dining requires a car (E).

**Vegetarian dining**
- **PRU (Michelin-starred):** a plant-based concept ("Plant. Raise. Understand") [S10|V2]. The Michelin write-up, however, says the menu centres on seafood and plants [S10|V2]. PRU relocated within the resort in 2023 [S9|V2].
- **Seafood at Trisara:** offers a "land or sea" tasting menu [S11|V2].
- **Gap:** a dedicated vegetarian menu is **U**. Request a vegetarian PRU menu when booking.

**Spa, romance and reviews**
- **Romance:** high. The private cove, a Michelin-starred restaurant and only 39 villas [S10|V2] make it a strong romantic choice.
- **Expedia rating:** 9.8/10 from 177 reviews [S1|V1].
- **Recent reviews:** no 2025 complaint pattern was found. One TripAdvisor stay from Sept 2024 was titled "rather average experience" [S4|V2].

**Deal-breakers**
- Price.
- Internet evidence is contradictory.
- Hillside layout means waiting for buggies.
- Swimmability of the beach is disputed.

**Price and terms**
- **Expedia:** **$11,327 total** for 6 nights (÷6 = $1,888; avg-nightly field $1,590; strikeout $17,116) [S1|V1, cheapest category, probably a single-room junior suite (E)].
- **Signature Ocean View Pool Suite:** price U.
- **Festive rules:** U.

### 2.3 Sri Panwa (Cape Panwa, Phuket)

**Brand and logistics**
- **Brand:** independent.
- **Area:** a hillside on the tip of Cape Panwa, southeast Phuket [S18|V2].
- **Transfer:** HKT is 36 km straight-line (E), roughly 50–70 min by road (E). No boat is needed.

**Recommended room: 1-Bedroom Pool Villa (ocean or garden view)**
- **Layout:** a **living room with pantry**, plus a sun deck and an ocean-view pavilion [S12|V2]. Another source describes a "spacious living room" and an outdoor pavilion [S13|V2].
- **Conflicting sizes:** one listing gives 250 m² for the 1-bedroom villas [S13]. Hotels.com lists a 70 m² 1-bedroom ocean-view unit and a 140 m² "1BR luxury residential pool villa" [S14]. **Confirm the exact villa type.**
- **Desk and work evidence:** a Phuket101 guest **used the living room as a workspace** [S15|V2, undated].
- **Private pool and view:** private pool. Ocean or garden view depending on category.

**Internet**
- **Insufficient evidence (U).** The only claim is "unlimited wireless broadband in villa", which is marketing copy [S12].

**Beach, noise and surroundings**
- **Beach:** a small private beach, and the resort sits on a hillside [S18|V2]. It is not a swimming-beach resort (E).
- **Noise risk:** Baba Nest rooftop bar (12 tables) and the DJ-led nightlife bring in "an international, hip and happening crowd" [S18|V2]. No noise complaints were found (U). **Ask for a villa away from Baba Nest and the pool club.**
- **Isolation:** moderate. Cape Panwa has local restaurants nearby, for example Live India [S17|V2].

**Vegetarian dining: good evidence**
- **On site:** Baba Poolclub has a **dedicated vegetarian and vegan menu** [S16|V2, undated].
- **Nearby:** Live India in Cape Panwa has "lots of vegetarian and vegan options" [S17|V2].

**Spa, romance and reviews**
- **Spa and romance:** Cool Spa (E). Sunset at Baba Nest [S18|V2].
- **Expedia rating:** 9.2/10 from 614 reviews [S1|V1].

**Deal-breakers**
- Party atmosphere on weekends.
- Longest airport transfer of the Phuket options.
- Villa sizes are inconsistent across sources.

**Price and terms**
- **Expedia:** **$2,974 total** for 6 nights (÷6 = $496; avg-nightly field $413; strikeout $5,224) [S1|V1, cheapest category, probably not the 1-bedroom villa (E)].
- **1-Bedroom Pool Villa:** price U.

### 2.4 InterContinental Phuket Resort (Kamala)

**Brand and logistics**
- **Brand:** IHG. Points can be earned and redeemed through **IHG One Rewards** [S1|V1].
- **Area:** Kamala Beach, west coast.
- **Transfer:** HKT is 17 km straight-line (E), roughly 30–40 min (E). No boat is needed.
- **Traffic:** traffic on the coastal road is a recurring complaint [S34|V2].

**Recommended rooms**
- **Best two-zone option:** the 2-Bedroom Pool Villa with Club Lounge access, about 260 m² (2,799 sq ft), with a **separate sitting area and separate dining area** [S33|V2].
- **Smaller option:** the 1-Bedroom Pool Villa, about 210 m². Whether it has a separate living room is U.
- **Club Suite (ocean view):** about 61 m², with a sitting area but no true separate room [S33|V2].
- **Backup workspace:** the Club Lounge (E).
- **Layout:** the pool villas are on the hillside side of the resort, reached by buggy or a tunnel [S34|V2].
- **Desk and chair:** U.

**Internet**
- **Insufficient evidence (U).**

**Beach, noise and surroundings**
- **Beach:** Kamala Beach. It is public, with a private hotel frontage, and the beach bar gets praise [S34|V2]. West-coast seas are calm in December [S53|V2].
- **Off-site dining:** Kamala village restaurants are within reach (E). This is the best option for walking out to eat.

**Vegetarian dining**
- **No specific evidence (U).** An international brand plus Kamala's restaurants suggests a moderate likelihood of good options (E).

**Reviews**
- **Ratings:** 9.6/10 from 329 reviews on Expedia [S1|V1]. Trip.com shows 9.2/10 from 217 reviews, with "no negative reviews in the past year" [S34|V2].
- **Praise:** service, breakfast.
- **Complaints:** a December 2025 reviewer found the connected pool not very clean. Restaurant prices are high [S34|V2].

**Deal-breakers**
- A large, busier resort with less romance than the boutique options (E).
- The cheap rate is a standard room.

**Price and terms**
- **Expedia:** **$5,767 total** for 6 nights (÷6 = $961; avg-nightly field $810) [S1|V1, cheapest category].

### 2.6 Rayavadee (Railay / Phra Nang, Krabi)

**Brand and logistics**
- **Brand:** independent.
- **Transfer:** KBV to Nong Nuch pier is about 20 min, then about 20 min by boat [S26|V2, undated]. A round-trip **shared speedboat from Nong Nuch Pier has run since 1 May 2026** [S31|V2]. **A boat is required.**
- **Boat risk:** December is the calm dry season on the Andaman coast [S53,S54|V2], so the risk of a cancelled crossing is low (E). Still, leave a buffer before the 24 Dec flight.
- **Onward flight:** from KBV to Bangkok (E; check schedules).
- **Arriving via Phuket instead** adds roughly 2.5–3 h by road (E).

**Recommended room: Deluxe Pavilion**
- **Size and layout:** about 90 m² (968 sq ft) [S27|V2], on **two storeys** [S25|V2]. A third guest sleeps on a sofa bed on the ground floor [S27|V2]. That implies a lounge downstairs and the bedroom upstairs (E), a natural split for two people on calls at once.
- **Higher tier:** the Rayavadee Villa has a separate building for living and dining [S32|V2, older].
- **Private pool:** standard pavilions have none (E). Pool pavilions exist, but their price is U.
- **Desk and chair:** U.

**Internet**
- **Insufficient evidence (U).** "Complimentary WiFi in all pavilions" is a listing claim [S25].
- **Mobile signal:** Railay is a limestone headland, so it **may fall back to 4G** (E) [S52|V2, generic caveat].

**Beach, noise and surroundings**
- **Beach:** Phra Nang beach is excellent in December, with a sea around 28–29 °C [S54|V2]. It is busy 10 am–4 pm with day-trippers [S29|V2; S28|V2].
- **Off-site dining:** limited upscale dining outside the resort [S28|V2].
- **Condition:** "starting to show its age" [S30|V2].
- **2026 works:** the main pool and Raitalay Terrace renovation was listed for 31 Aug–20 Oct 2026, with conflicting dates elsewhere [S31|V2]. It should be finished by December (E). **Confirm.**

**Vegetarian dining**
- Four restaurants plus a cave restaurant [S28|V2]. **No vegetarian evidence (U).**

**Spa, romance and reviews**
- **Romance:** very high (cave dining, beach, boat access).
- **Ratings:** Google 4.6 from 1,357 reviews [S30|V2].

**Deal-breakers**
- Boat access.
- Ageing product.
- Internet and mobile signal unknown.
- Not on Expedia, so no price.

**Price**
- **Not returned** by Expedia (two queries). This does not prove there is no availability.
- Direct rate: U.

## 5. Cross-cutting findings and actions

**1. Internet**
- No property has published or reviewed Mbps figures, so treat every one of them as U.
- Before paying, ask each shortlisted hotel to put three things in writing:
  - a villa-level speed test (screenshot) for the exact category booked;
  - whether the villa has its own access point on fibre;
  - whether a wired Ethernet port exists.
- Bring a travel router and two Thai eSIMs from different operators (AIS + True) as failover.
- True claims full network modernization in Phuket as of May 2025 [S51|V2, operator claim]. AIS claims more than 95% 4G/5G coverage across the south [S52|V2/U, attribution uncertain]. Limestone island areas may fall back to 4G (E).

**2. Festive rules**
- No compulsory Christmas Eve gala dinner was found for any of the six. An example from elsewhere in Phuket (Sinae) applies its compulsory Christmas gala to **stays on 24 Dec** [S48|V2].
- The last night of this trip is **23 Dec**, so a 24 Dec checkout should avoid such galas (E).
- Festive minimum stays seen elsewhere in Phuket start on 24 Dec (The Kee, 3 nights from 24 Dec) [V2 snippet]. This trip mostly falls before that window (E).
- New Year's Eve galas (for example, Banyan Tree Phuket at THB 20,000 per adult [S47]) are irrelevant to these dates.
- **Confirm with each hotel:** festive surcharges, whether breakfast is included, and cancellation terms. The cheapest Expedia rates are often non-refundable (U).

**3. December sea and weather**
- December is the dry, calm high season on the Andaman coast.
- Sea about 28–29 °C, around 72 mm of rain for the month, mostly brief showers [S53,S54|V2].
- Prices are peak-season.

**4. Onward travel on Thu 24 Dec**
- The Phuket options give the most flight choice to Bangkok (E).
- With Rayavadee, Banyan Tree Krabi or Phulay Bay, you fly from KBV (E). Rayavadee adds a boat leg, so allow at least 3 h before departure (E).

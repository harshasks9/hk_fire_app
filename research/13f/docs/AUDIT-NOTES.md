# Audit notes — data quality findings and how they are handled

Recorded as they were found. Each item names the check, the finding, and what the pipeline does about it.

| # | Check | Finding | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coverage of the SEC structured data sets | XML 13F filings begin late June 2013; the archive for filings made in 2013Q2 holds 87,715 holdings lines against ~1.5M for later quarters. The period ended 2013-03-31 is only partly present. | Analysis window starts with the period ended 2013-06-30 (first full cross-section). |
| 2 | VALUE unit (SEC switched thousands → dollars on 2023-01-03) | 86 universe filings accepted after the switch still reported thousands (notably T. Rowe Price through 2024Q2); 41 pre-switch filings reported dollars. | Per-filing unit check against the cross-sectional median implied price of the same CUSIPs; corrected and logged in `filings.unit_fix`. Same rule applied to all 306k filer-period summaries. |
| 3 | Acceptance timestamps | data.sec.gov returned acceptance timestamps for all 5,834 universe filings (no fallback to filing date was needed). 55 % of filings are accepted after 20:00 ET on the deadline day. | Execution at the first session (daily engine) / quarter end (quarterly engine) strictly after acceptance. |
| 4 | Duplicate / nested entities | Pershing Square Inc. (parent) and Pershing Square Capital Management (subsidiary) both filed 13F-HR for 2025Q2–2026Q1 with nested tables; Elliott, Appaloosa, Jana, Caxton, Greenlight/DME, Ruane Cunniff, Pennant, SAC/Point72 changed filing entity. | One manager id per firm; per (manager, period) the entity with the larger long-equity value is used; entity chains are listed in `data/universe.csv`. |
| 5 | Multiple lines per security in a filing | Positions are split across investment-discretion and other-manager lines (Berkshire reports ~2 lines per CUSIP). | Aggregated to one row per (filing, CUSIP, put/call flag). |
| 6 | Implied quarter-end prices | A single filer (CIK 1476223) reported a $922 billion value for 11,734 SPY shares in 2013Q2, which dominated a value-weighted median. 2.2 % of CUSIP-quarters show a > 5× quarter-on-quarter price change, almost all in thinly held names with inconsistent units. | Unweighted median across holders with positions ≥ $200k (≥ 3 of them), else across all holders; names whose holders disagree by > 3× (interquartile) are dropped for that quarter; isolated > 5× spikes with no split are set to missing. |
| 7 | Splits | Detected cross-sectionally from holders' share-count ratios (863 events). Verified: AAPL 7:1 (2014Q2) and 4:1 (2020Q3), NVDA 4:1 (2021Q3) and 10:1 (2024Q2), AMZN and GOOGL 20:1 (2022), TSLA 3:1 (2022Q3). Known miss: TSLA 5:1 (2020Q3) — holders traded so heavily around it that fewer than half show a ratio within 15 % of 5. | Share changes for undetected splits are flagged `split_suspect` and excluded from the increase/reduce signals; the spike filter removes the phantom price move. The number of excluded rows is reported in the event tables' N. |
| 8 | Delisted securities | A security that stops being reported has no further implied price (the final payout is unknown). | Forward returns use the last observed price and are flagged `truncated`; every event table is re-run with vanished names at −100 % (worst case) and both are reported. |
| 9 | Dividends | 13F-implied prices are ex-dividend price levels. | All returns are price returns; benchmarks are measured the same way from their own 13F lines. Factor alphas (total-return factors) are biased down by the portfolio's dividend yield net of the market's and are labelled accordingly. |
| 10 | Options | 609k rows are puts, calls or principal-amount positions in the universe filings. | Typed, kept, excluded from every directional signal; no conversion to delta exposure. |
| 11 | Amendments | 482 amendments (restatements and new-holdings additions) among 5,834 universe filings. | Signals come from the earliest-accepted original only; `amended_later` marks the 8 % of originals later corrected, for the sensitivity run. |

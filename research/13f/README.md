# Institutional investor intelligence from the top 100 13F filers

A point-in-time research pipeline over SEC Form 13F filings: who the most-followed institutional
investors are, what they did quarter by quarter, which of their disclosed decisions carried
predictive information once the filing was public, and what — if anything — an outside investor
could have earned from it after delays and costs.

Everything here is reproducible from primary sources. Nothing in `outputs/` was typed in by hand.

```
pipeline/        one script per step, in order; each is idempotent and cached
  config.py          paths, constants, contact/API settings (environment variables)
  sec_download.py    1   the 54 SEC quarterly 13F archives (2013Q2 → latest)
  universe_resolve.py 1b draft manager list → SEC filing entities (CIKs) with evidence; universe_build.py → data/universe.csv
  sec_load.py        2   archives → Parquet (holdings, all filers) + DuckDB (submissions, cover pages)
  sec_submissions.py 3   EDGAR acceptance timestamps for every universe filing
  security_master.py 4   CUSIP → ticker (OpenFIGI); sector_map.py 4b ticker → SIC → FF49 industry
  factors.py         5   Fama-French daily factors, momentum, 49 industries
  holdings_build.py  6   the normalised manager–security–quarter dataset (originals vs amendments, units, splits)
  filer_summary.py   7   every filer's positions per quarter (for Universe B and the implied prices)
  prices_13f.py      8   quarter-end prices implied by all filers' value ÷ shares, split-adjusted (no vendor needed)
  behaviour.py, signals.py   9   manager-quarter behaviour; manager- and stock-level signal events (S1–S15)
  themes.py, theme_stages.py 10  theme taxonomy membership; adoption stages; historical theme cases
  copy_returns.py, universe_b.py 11  copy-portfolio returns for every filer; the point-in-time Universe B
  bt_quarterly.py, event_study_q.py 12  the quarterly backtest engine and the event studies
  strategies_q.py    13  strategies A–J, the passive baseline and the three portfolio designs
  scorecard.py       14  manager scorecards (investor quality vs 13F signal quality, point-in-time)
  validation.py, extra_tests.py 15  train/test, FDR, leaderboard; cost/lag/exclusion/Universe B sensitivities; S13/S15
  intelligence.py    16  the current report inputs (latest complete quarter; early filings kept separate)
  attribution.py     17  event-level attribution and data-driven case studies
  report_build.py    18  REPORT-DATA.md, dashboard.html, manager_database.csv
  run_all.sh, run_final.sh   the full chain / everything downstream of the price panel
data/            the manager universe (A and B), theme taxonomy, small reference tables
docs/            data dictionary, methodology, audit notes
outputs/         tables and figures the report cites (small; regenerated by the pipeline)
```

Large data (`$HK13F_DATA`, default `research/13f/data/`) is not committed: ~5 GB of SEC archives,
~2 GB of Parquet, prices and caches. `make all` (or running the steps in order) rebuilds it.

Environment variables: `HK13F_SEC_CONTACT="Name email"` (required by SEC), optional `TIINGO_TOKEN` (the daily
engine in bt_core.py/prices.py; unused in this run), optional `OPENFIGI_API_KEY`, `HK13F_DATA`.

Deliverables: `outputs/EXECUTIVE-REPORT.md` (the report), `outputs/REPORT-DATA.md` (every cited table),
`outputs/manager_database.csv`, `outputs/dashboard.html`, `outputs/current/` (watchlists), `outputs/leaderboard.csv`.
